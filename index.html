BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few things to know

  1. One question. One best answer.Each correct answer earns 1 mark. No negative marking. Submit Question saves your answer on this device and opens the next question immediately. Online synchronization runs in the background; check the save status before closing the browser. Correctness is shown only after final exam submission.
  2. Review at your own pace.Save answers, move between questions, and flag any you want to revisit. Answers remain editable until final submission unless the question is locked after a tab change or loss of focus.
  3. Your progress stays with you.Answers save on this device and sync when connected. Reconnect and use the same access code to resume.
  4. Submit when you are ready.Check your unanswered and flagged questions before submitting. The exam submits automatically when your duration ends or the exam closes, whichever comes first.
  5. Use full screen.Click a login field or Full screen to enter full screen. Start / Resume checks full screen before starting the attempt, hiding browser tabs and the taskbar. If you exit, use Full screen to restore it. Your browser may require permission; system controls can still appear.
  6. Stay on the exam window.After your first interaction with the exam, switching tabs or moving focus away from its window permanently locks all questions you have viewed. Dismiss any browser sign-in prompts before answering. Its existing answer is kept; an unanswered question stays unanswered. You cannot select, change or clear its answer, even after returning or refreshing. You may continue to other questions.
